RailOpt Control Desk — simplified wireframes

For railway traffic Controllers on 8-hour day and night shifts. Blue, white and black only. Top icons are 50px. The four most-used actions sit in the centre of every home screen.

Wireframe only: layout and colour guide for rebuilding dashboard/. No data or code behind it.

Why it looks like this
  • One question answered first: what needs me right now? The count is the headline.
  • The four actions a Controller uses most are the only big buttons, always in the same centre spot, so hands and eyes don't travel. Keys 1–4 work too.
  • The top bar is only for moving between pages. 4 page icons plus "me", all 50px, with labels on hover.
  • Blue means "act here". Black means "look here". Nothing else competes.
  • One zone at a time (the Controller's own). The 17-zone list lives in the dropdown, not on the page.

One gentle sound when an urgent alert arrives. No flashing, no repeating alarm.

Why it looks like this
  • Urgency is shown by the word URGENT + a solid round icon + its own tab, not by colour. That keeps to blue/white/black and works for colour-blind staff.
  • Plain words ("Broken rail", "very likely to fail") replace codes like HIGH_PRIORITY_DEFECT and percentages.
  • One button per alert, always on the right, always the same size.
